## 8. The papers
How to fill in a card:
- Write only what the abstract or the full text says. If it says nothing, write "not reported"
- Write in the language of the report (English). Keep terms from other languages in parentheses
- **Evidence** is one of "unchecked / abstract only / full text checked". When the full text is checked, give the page for numbers and methods (p. 12)
- **Role in the manuscript** gives the IDs of the item in section 4, the claim in section 3 or the citation in section 5, and the relation (supports / conflicts / source of theory / method precedent / background). E.g. "K2, C1 supports", "R3 cited; opposite direction"
- Keep the first line short (the text after `**RQ**:` goes into the matrix). Put details in sub-bullets below it
