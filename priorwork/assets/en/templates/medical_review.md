## 9. The papers
How to fill in a card:
- Write only what the abstract or the full text says. If it says nothing, write "not reported"
- Write in the language of the report (English), keeping terms in the original in brackets
- **Evidence** is one of "unchecked / abstract only / full text checked". When the full text is checked, give the page (p. 12) for numbers and methods
- Give effect sizes as reported (HR, OR, RR, mean difference) with the 95% CI and the outcome they belong to
- Keep the first line to one short sentence (it goes into the matrix). Put details in sub-bullets below it
